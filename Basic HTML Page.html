    <p id="id1" class="para">

        Animal Farm by George Orwell is a story about a group of farm

        animals who become tired of being treated badly by their owner,

        Mr. Jones. They decide to rebel and remove him from the farm.


        The animals believe they can now live freely and equally,

        where no animal will be treated as less than another.

    </p>


    <h3><u>Story Summary:</u></h3>


    <p>

        At first, everything seems to go well. But slowly, the pigs,

        especially Napoleon, start taking more power.


        They change the rules according to their own needs and use

        propaganda to make the other animals believe that everything

        is fine.


        The animals work harder, but their lives do not really improve.

    </p>


    <pre>

The animals wanted freedom.

They dreamed of equality.

But slowly, their dreams started changing.

    </pre>

    <p>

        and that freedom and equality can be lost when people stop

        questioning those in authority.
